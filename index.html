Bootstrap confidence interval

Generate samples

A bootstrap sample draws n values from your data with replacement, then computes the statistic. Repeat that thousands of times and the middle 95% of those results is a 95% confidence interval.

Randomization test

Generate shuffles

If the groups really didn't differ, the labels A and B would be interchangeable. Each shuffle reassigns the labels at random and recomputes the difference. The p-value is how often a shuffle beats the difference you actually observed.
